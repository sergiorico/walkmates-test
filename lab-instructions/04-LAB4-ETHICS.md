# Lab 4 — Ethics & Sustainability

> Module **M6**. **Workshop-based — no repository or CI work required.** Submitted via **Canvas**.
> Due **wk 42**. Done in pairs.

## Learning objectives (DV033G)

Relate software quality to societal aspects — sustainability, equity, diversity, inclusion;
reason about the ethics of AI-augmented testing.

## What you'll do

### Clarification: follow the workshop activity

The expected work is what we practised during the workshop:

1. Work in **Miro** with the AI tools and their sustainability implications.
2. Identify **facts and effects using the green and red cards**.
3. Position the effects in the **sustainability pentagon**, considering the relevant dimensions
   and levels of impact. Connect related effects where possible.
4. Write a **short reflection on your findings**.

Use the workshop's five sustainability dimensions — **social, individual, environmental,
economic, technical** — and its levels of impact: **immediate, enabling, structural**.

**Use the workshop activities as your reference. You do not need to complete additional
activities beyond what we discussed during the session.** A separate ethics essay, a separate
equity/diversity/inclusion task, and a completed reflection-template questionnaire are not
additional requirements. You do not need to start a new analysis of WalkMates or another system.

## Submission

Upload **one PDF** containing your **workshop material** (the cards and sustainability
pentagon/map, exported or captured from Miro) and a **short reflection on your findings** to
**Canvas**. Explain the effects you identified, their dimensions and levels of impact, and
any connections you made. No separate additional analysis is required.

You may write in Word or another tool. The [`reflection template`](reflection-template.md)
is optional guidance; you do not need to answer every template question.
`reflections/lab4-reflection.md` is an optional Markdown location, not a required extra file.
No public reflection, repository, or CI part is required. Follow the Canvas assignment for
deadlines and examination opportunities.
