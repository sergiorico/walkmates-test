# Lab 4 — Ethics & Sustainability

> Module **M6**. **Paper-based — the one lab not in the repo.** Submitted via **Canvas**.
> Due **wk 42**. Done in pairs.

## Learning objectives (DV033G)

Relate software quality to societal aspects — sustainability, equity, diversity, inclusion;
reason about the ethics of AI-augmented testing.

## What you'll do

1. **Apply the Sustainability Awareness Framework (SUsAF)** to a software/AI system of your
   choice — or to **WalkMates itself**. Map impacts across the five SUsAF dimensions —
   **social, individual, environmental, economic, technical** — and include at least one
   **chain of effects** (a short-term benefit that creates a longer-term risk). *Example seed for
   WalkMates: the AI "explain this match" feature improves individual experience (individual),
   but running LLM calls at scale has an environmental and economic cost, and over-trusting its
   explanations could erode tester/seeker judgment (social/technical).*
2. **Ethics of AI-augmented testing.** Take a concrete trade-off from this course — e.g. the cost
   (energy/money) of running mutation testing and LLM calls at scale, or AI **displacing tester
   judgment** — and argue a reasoned position.
3. *(Optional)* **Equity/inclusion:** who benefits and who is at risk from a quality failure in
   your chosen system (e.g. a phone-format or identity-validation rule that excludes some users)?

## Submission

A short paper-based analysis (SUsAF map + discussion) **+** the completed
`reflections/lab4-reflection.md`, created from the
[`reflection template`](reflection-template.md), via **Canvas**. No repo/CI part.
